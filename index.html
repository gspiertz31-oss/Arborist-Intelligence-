Arborist Guides

Practical reference topics for tree work and professional development.

🌿 Pruning & Tree Biology

Understand branch attachment, pruning objectives, wound response and the effects of poor cuts.

  • Identify the branch collar and branch bark ridge.
  • Choose cuts that suit the pruning objective.
  • Avoid unnecessary wounds and excessive canopy removal.
  • Do not assume wound paint or sealant is beneficial for every tree.

🧗 Climbing & Rigging

Plan work around tree structure, loads, equipment ratings and changing site conditions.

  • Inspect climbing and rigging equipment before use.
  • Assess anchor points and potential failure modes.
  • Use a task-specific rescue and communication plan.
  • Never exceed equipment ratings or improvise load systems.
Rigging failures can be fatal. Practical work requires appropriate training, supervision and site-specific planning.

🪚 Chainsaws

Maintenance and safe operating principles.

  • Check the chain brake, chain tension and lubrication.
  • Use the correct chain, file size and sharpening angles for the model.
  • Inspect the bar, sprocket and safety equipment.
  • Understand compression, tension and kickback zones before cutting.

Follow the manufacturer's manual and current training requirements.

🧰 Equipment Care

Protect the gear your life may depend on.

  • Follow manufacturer instructions for cleaning and drying ropes and harnesses.
  • Inspect for cuts, glazing, chemical damage and abnormal wear.
  • Remove damaged or suspect equipment from service.
  • Use only manufacturer-approved lubricants and maintenance methods.

💪 Your Body Is the Tool

Reduce avoidable strain through planning and good work habits.

  • Vary tasks and take recovery breaks.
  • Avoid repeatedly working in awkward positions where possible.
  • Use suitable lifting and handling techniques.
  • Seek professional advice for persistent pain, weakness, numbness or injury.

⚡ Powerline Awareness

Electrical requirements vary by country, network operator, voltage and task.

  • Identify the network owner and applicable rules.
  • Confirm the required minimum approach distances from authoritative sources.
  • Arrange authorised utility involvement when required.
  • Never rely on generic online guidance to judge safe distances.

🌱 Soil, Botany & Fertiliser

Learn how roots, soil conditions and nutrients affect tree performance.

  • Assess drainage, compaction, soil texture and root-zone conditions.
  • Consider pH and nutrient testing before recommending fertiliser.
  • Avoid fertilising a stressed tree without understanding the cause.
  • Protect roots and soil structure during site works.

Research & Knowledge Updates

A foundation for a source-aware knowledge system.

Current status: This webpage does not independently browse the internet, call an AI service, or update its database automatically. Those functions require a secure backend, connected research sources and a scheduled process.

1. Research a topic

Describe what needs researching. Generate a structured research brief to use with a research tool or AI service.

2. Verify before publishing

  • Prefer government, university, research-journal and recognised industry sources.
  • Record source title, author or organisation, URL and publication date.
  • Distinguish research findings from opinion and uncertain claims.
  • Check regional applicability, current legislation and safety guidance.
  • Have high-risk advice reviewed by a suitably qualified person.

3. Proposed update workflow

  1. Search approved sources on a schedule.
  2. Identify new findings or outdated information.
  3. Compare evidence and preserve citations.
  4. Prepare proposed changes for review.
  5. Publish approved changes with version history.

This is a planned workflow, not an active automated service.
