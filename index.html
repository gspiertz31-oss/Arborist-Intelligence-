Tips for useful photographs

  • Photograph the whole tree and its growing environment.
  • Take clear close-ups of both sides of affected leaves.
  • Include bark, branches, flowers or fruit when available.
  • Photograph the boundary between healthy and affected tissue.
  • Record location, season and when symptoms first appeared.

Arborist Field Guides

Pruning principles

Identify the branch collar and bark ridge. Plan cuts to preserve branch attachment and minimise unnecessary wounds. Avoid indiscriminate topping.

Tree care

Tree health assessment

Record crown condition, deadwood, cavities, cracks, fungal growth, root-zone disturbance and site conditions. Symptoms alone do not prove a tree is dangerous.

Inspection

Climbing & rigging

Assess anchors, equipment, loads, communication, exclusion zones and rescue arrangements. Complex work needs a competent, site-specific plan.

Work planning

Equipment maintenance

Follow manufacturer instructions for inspection, cleaning, storage and retirement. Quarantine damaged climbing equipment and defective safety devices.

Equipment

Powerline safety

Requirements depend on the network, voltage and task. Contact the relevant network operator and follow its current rules before work near electrical conductors.
